### Cybernetics & Robotics Student @ NTNU

I'm a **Cybernetics and Robotics** student at the **Norwegian University of Science and Technology (NTNU)**, interested in the intersection of **control systems, robotics, autonomous systems, and software**.

I enjoy understanding how things work from the mathematical model all the way to a working implementation — whether that means designing a controller, estimating a system's state, or writing the software that makes a robotic system operate in real time.
